Webhook retry backoff and duplicate delivery control

Version 3.2. Support visibility: internal. Error codes: WS-WEBHOOK-429, WS-WEBHOOK-5XX, and WS-DUP-EVENT.

Symptom

The customer sees repeated webhook deliveries, HTTP 429/5xx storm, or business side effects executed twice for one upstream event.

Diagnostic order

  1. Read the delivery log for attempt count, next retry time, and terminal status. Default backoff is exponential: 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, then dead-letter.
  2. Confirm the consumer returns 2xx only after durable commit. Returning 200 before local write causes safe retries to look like duplicates.
  3. Require consumers to deduplicate by event identifier. Northstar Workspace guarantees at-least-once delivery, not exactly-once.
  4. For HTTP 429, check consumer rate limits and pause subscription only when approved by a support lead.
  5. Do not enable bulk replay across an entire day from the agent console. Bulk replay is a high-risk operation and must go through HITL.

Conflict note

If one document recommends immediate bulk replay and another forbids it without approval, treat the conflict as unresolved evidence and abstain from automatic bulk actions.

Safety boundary

Service credits offered because of webhook outages are financial actions. Agents may draft the request, but grant_service_credit must enter HITL with evidence_ids.

Success criteria

Retry storm stops, dead-lettered events are replayed one-by-one with stable event identifiers, and the customer confirms exactly one business effect per event.
